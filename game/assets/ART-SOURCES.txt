THE UNFOLDING — ORIGINAL GAME ART

All creature, guardian and celestial-sculpture geometry is original procedural work
in game/creatures.js and game/project-stars.js. No third-party game models are used.

BEACN
Public user-owned logo, downloaded from the user's requested site on 2026-09-30:
https://beacn.space/images/logo-dark-mode-with-word-no-bg.png
Home page visually inspected: https://beacn.space/
Logo: thin ivory/cyan outer circle, two separated inner arcs, outlined lowercase
wordmark. A faithful original 3D interpretation is used as the central landmark.
The PNG is only used for its small exact-logo plaque.

HEARD
Home page visually inspected: https://heard-us.vercel.app/
Ivory serif wordmark, navy/gold palette and “Your Voice Matters” are the source
identity. The orbital civic-signal sculpture is an original interpretation.

TYPEFACES

THE AGENT, THE ASTER, FLORA AND LANDMARKS
Original models generated procedurally with Blender 5 from the scripts in
art/blender/ (agent.py, ship.py, flora.py, landmarks_a.py, landmarks_b.py) and
optimized with gltfpack into public/models/. The agent is an original
hero-shooter style character ("MAX // AGENT"): no existing game character,
costume or silhouette is reproduced. No third-party models, textures or
motion capture are used; all animation is keyframed in the scripts.

CREATURES
Sentinels and the five world wardens remain original procedural geometry in
game/creatures.js.

AUDIO
All music and sound effects are synthesized live with the Web Audio API
(game/audio/). No samples or recordings are used.
